$AKTX Akari Therapeutics PLC  ·  阿卡里生技 BIOTECH HOT +120~210%
Tape: Close 待確認 (+120%~210% 報導不一) · Vol: 爆量 est. >20x 30D avg · Range 待確認 · 前日 AH +157% 觸發 · Catalyst: May 22–23 連二日熱炒
為什麼今天熱 / Why It's Hot

Akari Therapeutics（AKTX）在 5/22 發布其 KRAS 突變型胰臟癌臨床前數據，主力藥物 AKTX-101（TROP2 ADC）在與 KRAS 抑制劑 adagrasib 合用時展現「協同殺腫瘤」效果，而市面上主流的 Topo I 靶向 TROP2 ADC 類藥物反而呈現拮抗效果。AKTX-101 的差異在於其 RNA spliceosome 調節 payload（PH1）——這在 KRAS 突變癌症領域屬 first-in-class 機制。同日公司還完成 $5.5M 私募融資，雙重催化劑點燃投機性買盤，盤後即跳漲 89% → 157%。

Akari Therapeutics released preclinical data on May 22 showing AKTX-101, its TROP2 ADC, demonstrated synergistic tumor-killing activity when combined with KRAS inhibitor adagrasib in pancreatic cancer models — while rival Topo I-based TROP2 ADCs showed antagonism. The differentiation lies in its RNA spliceosome-modulating payload (PH1) that degrades pre-mRNA transcripts carrying KRAS mutations. Paired with a $5.5M private placement closed the same day, the dual catalyst ignited a speculative biotech squeeze.

IV spiked sharply (exact figures unconfirmed). With a small float, any high-volume catalyst can accelerate a gamma squeeze dynamic. Short interest data pending from Fintel/Ortex. The $5.5M placement price versus market creates dilution-related selling risk post-lockup.

"AKTX-101 synergy with KRAS is genuinely differentiated if it holds in humans"

胰臟癌 KRAS 突變佔超過 90%，是腫瘤學最難啃的領域之一。近年 sotorasib、adagrasib 等 KRAS inhibitor 獲 FDA 核准，讓市場對 KRAS 聯療組合高度敏感。AKTX 屬微型股（micro cap），這類股在生技消息面前常有 3–10 倍暴漲，但 preclinical data 到人體試驗仍有極高失敗率。

· 同類 ADC 股（IMVT、KDNY）可能出現 spillover buzz
