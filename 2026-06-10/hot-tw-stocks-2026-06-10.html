為什麼今天熱 / Why it's hot

Citi issued a research note Jun 9 raising its target from NT$495 to NT$1,500 (3×+ in one step), citing 2028 EPS above NT$43 and a re-rated 40× P/E reflecting the structural AI passive component supercycle. This single catalyst ignited a sector-wide chain reaction. High-end MLCC lead times have stretched to 12–16 weeks (some specs to 6 months), meaning price hikes are real, not speculative.
