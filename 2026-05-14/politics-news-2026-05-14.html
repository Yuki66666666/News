背景脈絡 / Context
兩國領導人上次峰會為2025年10月釜山，川普當時承諾2026年4月訪中，但伊朗戰爭（2026年2月28日美以聯合空襲啟動「史詩狂怒行動」）打亂原定行程，延至本週。此前雙方已就「美中貿易委員會」和「投資委員會」框架達成工作級共識，據分析最可能宣布的成果包括波音飛機、黃豆等大宗採購協議及科技出口管制的局部鬆綁。The two leaders last met at the Busan Summit in October 2025. Trump promised then to visit China in April 2026, but the U.S.-Israel military campaign against Iran ("Operation Epic Fury," launched Feb 28) forced postponement. Working-level talks had already sketched a "Board of Trade" and "Board of Investment" framework; expected deliverables include Chinese purchases of Boeing aircraft and soybeans, plus partial tech-export relief.
