Anthropic 完成 650 億美元 H 輪融資，估值逼近 1 兆美元，超越 OpenAI 成業界第一
Anthropic Closes $65B Series H, Valuation Nears $1 Trillion, Overtaking OpenAI

來源 / Source: Crunchbase News · 2026-05-29 · TechCrunch
核心事實

Anthropic 完成 Series H 融資，募得 650 億美元，投後估值達 9,650 億美元，正式超越 OpenAI，成為全球市值最高的 AI 新創公司。同一週，Andrej Karpathy（OpenAI 共同創辦人、前 Tesla AI 負責人）宣布加入 Anthropic，在 pretraining 負責人 Nick Joseph 麾下組建新團隊，聚焦以 Claude 自身加速預訓練研究。

Anthropic closed its Series H at $65 billion, pushing its post-money valuation to approximately $965 billion and surpassing OpenAI. In the same week, Andrej Karpathy — OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI lead — joined Anthropic's pretraining team under Nick Joseph to build a new group focused on using Claude to accelerate pretraining research.

背景脈絡

Anthropic 成立於 2021 年，由前 OpenAI 核心成員 Dario Amodei 領軍，長期以「AI 安全優先」為品牌核心。此輪融資之前，Google 與 Broadcom 已宣布與 Anthropic 擴大算力合作夥伴關係，提供多 GW 等級的次世代運算資源。Karpathy 此前曾短暫創業（Eureka Labs），並以教學影片在開發者社群中擁有極高聲望，其加入被外界視為 Anthropic 在 frontier talent 爭奪戰中的重大勝利。

Anthropic, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI leadership, has consistently positioned safety as its differentiator. Prior to this round, Google and Broadcom had already announced an expanded compute partnership with the company. Karpathy's move is seen as a significant talent signal in the frontier model race.

影響觀察

接近 1 兆美元的估值意味著 Anthropic 在下一輪融資或潛在 IPO 時，可能需要提交堪比大型上市科技公司的財務指標。Karpathy 以 Claude 加速預訓練的思路，呼應了業界對「用 AI 做 AI 研究（AI for AI R&D）」的高度期待，若有突破，將對競品的訓練成本與週期產生結構性壓力。

A near-trillion-dollar valuation raises the bar for Anthropic's next capital event. Karpathy's mandate — using Claude to accelerate pretraining — directly embodies the "AI for AI R&D" thesis; a meaningful breakthrough here could structurally compress competitors' training timelines and costs.

GitHub Copilot 今日正式切換為 Token 計費，部分開發者面臨帳單暴增 10–50 倍
GitHub Copilot Switches to Token-Based Billing Today; Some Devs Face 10–50x Cost Spike

來源 / Source: GitHub Blog · 2026-06-01 · TechJournal
核心事實

2026 年 6 月 1 日起，所有 GitHub Copilot 方案（個人 Pro $10/月、Pro+ $39/月）全面轉換為用量計費（usage-based billing），以 AI Credits 計算：1 credit = $0.01 美元，用量依 input/output/cached token 消耗折算。代碼補全與 Next Edit Suggestions 維持無限制；其餘功能（包括 Agent mode、代碼審查）均計費。多位開發者回報，重度使用 agentic 工作流的月帳單從 $29 跳升至 $750，甚至更高。

Starting June 1, all Copilot plans moved to token-based AI Credits billing ($0.01/credit). Code completions remain unlimited; all other features including agent mode and code review are now metered. Developers in heavy agentic workflows report bills jumping from $29 to $750+ per month.

背景脈絡

Microsoft 做出此決定的背景是：Copilot 複雜 coding session 的推論成本大幅攀升，舊有「吃到飽」訂閱模式已不可持續。這也反映出整個 AI 工具產業的共同矛盾——模型能力愈強，每次互動成本愈高，但用戶的付費習慣仍停留在訂閱時代。

Microsoft made this move as inference costs for complex agentic sessions made the flat-fee model unsustainable — a tension the entire AI tools industry faces as models grow more capable and expensive to run.

影響觀察

此舉可能觸發大量 Copilot 用戶轉向 Cursor、Windsurf 等對手，或促使企業重新評估 AI coding 工具的 ROI。長期而言，「inference cost 終究會顯性化」這一現實將影響所有訂閱式 AI 產品的商業模式設計，值得密切追蹤用戶留存率。

This is likely to accelerate migration to competitors like Cursor and Windsurf, and force enterprises to model AI coding ROI more rigorously. More broadly, it signals that "inference cost is eventually someone's problem" — and that flat-fee AI subscriptions may be structurally unsustainable at high capability levels.

OpenAI Files Confidential S-1 IPO Prospectus, Targeting $852B–$1T Valuation for Sep 2026 Debut

背景脈絡

OpenAI 已完成史上最大規模私募融資（1,220 億美元，投後估值 8,520 億美元），此次 IPO 若成功將是科技史上最大規模的上市案之一。公司此前已從非營利架構轉型為「公益公司（PBC）」，為公開市場投資者鋪路。與此同時，Anthropic 估值逼近 1 兆美元，對 OpenAI 形成直接壓力。

OpenAI previously closed the largest private venture round in history at $122B and converted to a Public Benefit Corporation structure, clearing the path for a public listing. Anthropic's near-trillion valuation creates direct competitive optics pressure.

影響觀察

龐大的虧損與資本需求意味著 IPO 定價將是一場「成長故事 vs. 財務現實」的市場考驗。若 OpenAI 能在上市前展示 AGI 或重大能力突破，可能支撐高估值；反之，若公開市場對 AI 估值邏輯趨於保守，將對整個 AI 一級市場估值產生下行壓力。

The IPO is a direct test of whether public markets will apply AI-era growth multiples to a company burning $14B/year. A successful debut could set a high-water mark for AI valuations broadly; a disappointing one could reprice the entire sector.

Effort control could become the standard knob for cost-conscious agentic developers. SEC filing integration signals Anthropic's push into enterprise financial workflows — a market segment adjacent to OpenAI's current enterprise focus.

模型

Inception 的 Mercury 2：擴散架構推理模型每秒輸出 1,009 tokens，比主流模型快 5 倍以上
Mercury 2 by Inception: Diffusion-Based Reasoning LLM Hits 1,009 Tokens/s, 5x+ Faster Than Rivals

來源 / Source: Inception Labs Blog · 2026-02-24 · The Decoder
核心事實

Inception Labs 於今年 2 月推出 Mercury 2，這是首個具備推理能力的擴散式大型語言模型（dLLM）。Mercury 2 放棄逐 token 生成的 Transformer 架構，改為同步精煉多個文字區塊，在 Nvidia Blackwell GPU 上實測達 1,009 tokens/秒——對比 Claude 4.5 Haiku Reasoning 的約 89 tokens/秒與 GPT-5 Mini 的約 71 tokens/秒。定價為每百萬 input tokens $0.25、output tokens $0.75，顯著低於 Gemini 3 Flash。

Mercury 2 is the first diffusion-based reasoning LLM, generating ~1,009 tokens/s vs ~89 for Claude Haiku Reasoning and ~71 for GPT-5 Mini. Priced at $0.25/$0.75 per million input/output tokens — substantially cheaper than Gemini 3 Flash.

背景脈絡

擴散語言模型（dLLM）此前被認為在推理一致性上不及 autoregressive 模型，Mercury 2 是目前首個在推理任務上達到可與主流競品比肩的 dLLM，其「並行精煉」的生成方式使得延遲結構完全不同，特別適合需要低延遲的場景。

Diffusion LLMs were long thought to trail autoregressive models on reasoning tasks. Mercury 2 is the first to credibly challenge that assumption, with a fundamentally different latency profile suited for voice and agentic applications.

影響觀察

若 dLLM 的推理品質繼續追趕 Transformer 模型，其天然的高速低成本將對即時語音助理、代碼補全等延遲敏感市場形成顛覆性壓力，也意味著 Nvidia Blackwell 加速卡在推論端的需求可能進一步擴大。

If dLLM reasoning quality continues closing the gap, the architecture's inherent speed advantage could disrupt latency-sensitive markets — voice, real-time coding, agentic loops — and expand Blackwell's inference-market addressable footprint.

arXiv Paper: "AI Can Learn Scientific Taste" — Models Outperform GPT-5.2 on Research Evaluation

來源 / Source: arXiv 2603.14473 · 2026-03
核心事實

近期一篇 arXiv 論文提出「Scientific Judge」模型，在評估科學論文品質上大幅超越 GPT-5.2 與 Gemini 3 Pro 等強力基線，並具備跨時間段、跨領域與跨同儕審查偏好的泛化能力。配套的「Scientific Thinker」模型提出的研究想法，在同行盲評中被評定影響力顯著高於基線模型，顯示「科學品味」是可學習的客觀目標。

A recent paper trains a "Scientific Judge" model that substantially outperforms GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro on evaluating paper quality, generalizing across time, field, and review preference. A companion "Scientific Thinker" model generates ideas rated higher-impact than baselines, suggesting scientific taste is a learnable objective.

背景脈絡

AI 輔助科研（AI for Science）一直被認為最難突破的環節是「如何判斷什麼研究值得做」——此前普遍認為這屬於純人類直覺領域。這篇論文直接挑戰這一假設。

Identifying which research is worth doing has long been considered a purely human intuition. This paper directly challenges that assumption and is the first credible demonstration of learnable scientific judgment at scale.

影響觀察

若 Scientific Judge/Thinker 方法成熟，可望深度改變學術同儕審查流程、AI 公司的研究方向優先化，乃至創投在科技類別上的盡職調查方式。這也讓「AI 自動化 AI 研究」的議題更具體化，令業界對 AI 加速科研的期待更為現實。

A mature version of this capability could reshape peer review, how AI labs prioritize research directions, and even how VCs evaluate deep tech. It makes the "AI automating AI research" thesis more concrete than ever.

Groq Raises $650M for Inference Cloud Pivot After Nvidia's $20B "Not-Acqui-Hire"

AI 晶片新創 Groq 正在向現有投資人募集 6.5 億美元，用於擴展推論雲業務。此次融資背景是：2025 年 12 月，Nvidia 以 200 億美元技術授權協議，取走 Groq 多名高階工程師並取得其硬體技術授權；現任代理 CEO Adam Winter 與 CFO Matt Eng 正在重新定位公司，主攻推論即服務（inference-as-a-service）市場。現有大股東 Disruptive 與 Infinitium 已承諾補足其他投資人未認購的份額，相當於保證全額到位。

Groq is raising $650M from existing backers to pivot to inference-cloud after Nvidia's December 2025 $20B deal that took senior engineers and hardware IP. Guarantor investors Disruptive and Infinitium have backstopped the full round.

背景脈絡

Groq 以自研 LPU（Language Processing Unit）起家，以超高速推論聞名，但在硬體事業受 Nvidia 整合後，公司需要找到新的價值主張。此次轉型與 Cloudflare、Together AI 等「推論雲」玩家的方向一致，但 Groq 可利用其對自身硬體的深度了解作為差異化優勢。

Groq built its reputation on ultra-fast LPU inference. Post-Nvidia deal, pivoting to inference cloud is the logical path to monetize remaining IP and infrastructure — competing in a space increasingly crowded by Cloudflare AI, Together AI, and Fireworks AI.

影響觀察

Groq 若成功建立推論雲，將成為 Nvidia 既是供應商又是潛在對手的微妙競合關係；同時也顯示推論市場的需求之大，足以同時支撐多個專業玩家存在，對超大規模雲端廠商（AWS、Azure、GCP）的 AI 推論業務構成新壓力。

Groq's pivot places it in a co-opetitive relationship with Nvidia — a customer of its IP while also building a competing inference product. If successful, it validates the thesis that inference-cloud is large enough to support multiple specialist players alongside hyperscalers.

Google I/O 2026: Redesigned Search Box, Ask YouTube Launch — AI Agents Now the Core UX

Google I/O 2026 was dominated by AI. Google redesigned its iconic search box for complex AI agent tasks, and YouTube launched "Ask YouTube" for Q&A-to-video navigation. Google framed the entire I/O as a full AI rebuild across its product portfolio.

The core tension: agentic search that answers questions directly could cannibalize ad click revenue. How Google threads the "answer vs. ad" needle will be one of the most important business model experiments of H2 2026. Ask YouTube is a smart moat-building move using Google's video dominance.

政策與監管 / Policy & Regulation

政策

科羅拉多州大幅削弱 AI Act 並延後至 2027 年；紐約 RAISE Act 轉為強制框架揭露，今日起進入倒數
Colorado Guts and Delays Its AI Act to 2027; NY RAISE Act Targets Frontier Devs, Effective Jan 2027

來源 / Source: Consumer Finance Monitor · 2026-05-12 · JDSupra
核心事實

科羅拉多州長 Polis 於 5 月 14 日簽署 SB 189，大幅改寫原 Colorado AI Act：刪除防止演算法歧視的注意義務、部署者風險管理程式要求及衝擊評估規定，僅保留以自動決策技術（ADMT）為主的揭露與透明度義務，生效日期從 2026 年 6 月 30 日延後至 2027 年 1 月 1 日。與此同時，紐約 RAISE Act（2025 年 12 月簽署、2026 年 3 月章節修正完成）要求年收入逾 5 億美元、訓練算力逾 10²⁶ FLOPs 的 frontier model 開發者，須公開安全協議、72 小時回報事故，並在金融服務部下設立監理辦公室，2027 年 1 月 1 日生效。

Colorado's SB 189 (signed May 14) guts its landmark AI Act — removing anti-discrimination duty of care and impact assessment requirements, keeping only ADMT disclosure rules, and delaying to Jan 1, 2027. New York's RAISE Act targets frontier developers ($500M+ revenue, 10²⁶+ FLOPs) with safety framework disclosure and 72-hour incident reporting, effective Jan 1, 2027.

背景脈絡

兩州的法規演變均反映出聯邦政府的施壓：2025 年 12 月，Trump 行政令明確要求挑戰與聯邦 AI 政策相衝突的州法，並以聯邦撥款為槓桿迫使各州配合。科羅拉多撤退，紐約則選擇以「透明度」切入，避開與聯邦正面衝突，同時在 frontier model 層面保住監管話語權。

Both shifts reflect the Trump executive order's federal preemption pressure. Colorado retreated; New York found a narrower but harder-to-challenge angle — transparency and incident reporting for the largest models only.

影響觀察

此兩案揭示了後 Trump 行政令時代美國 AI 監管的新格局：要求開發者「揭露安全框架」遠比要求「防止歧視」更難被聯邦法院阻止。Anthropic、OpenAI 等 frontier model 公司若年收入超過 5 億美元，需在未來六個月內制定符合 RAISE Act 的合規路徑。EU AI Act 透明度規定（8 月 2 日全面生效）同步施加壓力，跨國合規成本將大幅上升。

The emerging US state-level pattern: transparency and incident reporting are more legally defensible than anti-discrimination mandates. Frontier labs with $500M+ revenue now face a compliance clock for both NY RAISE Act and EU AI Act (fully applicable Aug 2). Cross-border compliance costs are set to rise materially.

觀點與深度 / Opinion & Deep Reads

觀點

前線競賽已成三足鼎立：Google 如何打 AI 戰爭
The Frontier is Now a Three-Way Tie: How Google Plans to Win the AI War

來源 / Source: Axios · 2026-05-21
核心事實

Axios 深度報導指出，Google、OpenAI 與 Anthropic 三家公司的高階主管，已普遍以「三方拉鋸（neck-and-neck）」描述當前前線模型競賽，各家在成本、速度與算力資源上做出不同取捨，而非存在明確的技術領先者。

Axios reports that executives at Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic now uniformly describe the frontier race as "neck-and-neck," with differentiation occurring at the level of cost/speed/compute tradeoffs rather than outright capability leadership.

背景脈絡

兩年前，業界普遍認為 OpenAI 在技術上遠超同儕。2026 年的局面是：Gemini 3 系列、Claude Opus 4.x 與 GPT-5 系列在多數基準測試上交替領先，差距極為細微，真正的競爭已轉移至生態系整合、定價與開發者體驗層面。
