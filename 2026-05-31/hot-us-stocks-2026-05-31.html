Replimune was left for dead in early May after a second FDA Complete Response Letter rejected RP1 for advanced melanoma. But on 5/29, the company announced FDA alignment on a BLA resubmission path, with FDA pledging to treat the resubmission as an "urgent matter." Rumors that FDA Commissioner Makary — who oversaw the rejection — may be fired added political fuel. REPL spiked from ~$3 to ~$9 in one of biotech's sharpest reversal moves of the year.
