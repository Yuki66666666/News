Anthropic 以 $965B 估值完成 $65B Series H，躍升全球最高估值 AI 新創 / Anthropic Closes $65B Series H at $965B Valuation, Overtaking OpenAI

來源 / Source: Anthropic Official · 2026-05-28 · TechCrunch 報導 · CNBC 報導
核心事實

Anthropic 於 5 月 28 日宣布完成 Series H 輪融資，融資金額 $65B，投後估值達 $965B，超越 OpenAI 的 $852B，成為全球估值最高的 AI 新創。本輪由 Altimeter Capital、Dragoneer、Greenoaks、Sequoia Capital 共同領投，Baillie Gifford、Blackstone、Brookfield、DST Global、Fidelity，以及三星、SK Hynix、Micron 等策略夥伴跟投。公司當前年化營收已突破 $47B，並預計本季度實現首次季度盈利。

Anthropic announced a $65B Series H on May 28, led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia at a $965B post-money valuation — surpassing OpenAI's $852B private mark. Revenue run rate crossed $47B this month; the company projects Q2 2026 operating profit of ~$559M, its first ever.

背景脈絡

Anthropic 成立於 2021 年，由前 OpenAI 研究副總裁 Dario Amodei 與 Daniela Amodei 領軍，始終以「安全優先」為核心差異化定位。Series H 是該公司的第八輪融資，距 Series G（$30B，估值 $380B）僅約五個月，顯示市場對其 Claude 產品線的企業需求高度信任。Bristol Myers Squibb 於本月稍早宣布全集團導入 Claude Enterprise，亦為此輪估值提供佐證。

Founded in 2021 by ex-OpenAI alumni, Anthropic has closed eight rounds in five years, with the valuation roughly doubling every six months in 2026. Enterprise traction — most visibly the Bristol Myers Squibb deal announced May 20 — has validated its B2B moat and its safety-first positioning ahead of what looks like a pre-IPO round.

影響觀察

$965B 估值重塑了整個 AI 一級市場的定錨點，下輪融資或 IPO 勢必以 $1T 為心理門檻。三星、SK Hynix、Micron 的加入說明晶片廠正在透過股權投資鎖定「護城河」，而非純粹依賴代工訂單。投資人最需要追蹤的後續事件：Anthropic 是否跟進 OpenAI 申請 IPO、本季度盈利能否持續，以及 Claude Mythos 的商業化路徑。

The $965B anchor resets private AI valuations. The chip-maker participation (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) signals a new equity-for-supply dynamic replacing pure purchase orders. Key things to watch: whether Anthropic files its own IPO within 12 months, whether Q2 profitability proves repeatable, and how Mythos gets commercialized beyond Project Glasswing.

OpenAI 秘密提交 IPO 申請，目標 9 月掛牌、估值衝破 $1T / OpenAI Files Confidential S-1, Eyes September IPO at $1T+ Valuation

來源 / Source: CNBC · 2026-05-20 · Fortune 深度分析
核心事實

OpenAI 於 5 月 22 日以「秘密申報」方式向美國 SEC 提交 S-1 草稿，由高盛與摩根士丹利主導承銷，目標 9 月正式掛牌，尋求超過 $1T 市值。公司 2026 年 2 月年化營收為 $25B（年增約 130%），但 CFO Sarah Friar 警告收入增速略低於內部目標，可能影響五年內高達 $600B 的資料中心及晶片支出計畫。

OpenAI filed a confidential draft S-1 with the SEC on May 22, targeting a September debut above $1T market cap. Current private valuation: $852B. Annualized revenue as of February: $25B. CFO Sarah Friar has flagged that sluggish revenue growth may constrain the company's $600B, five-year semiconductor and data-center commitment.

背景脈絡

「秘密申報」機制允許公司在 SEC 私下審查草稿後，才正式向公眾揭露 S-1，這是大型科技公司常見的 IPO 路徑（Airbnb、Robinhood 均採用此法）。OpenAI 此前已完成從非營利到有限利潤（capped-profit）到近期公益公司（PBC）的三度公司結構轉型，任何一層架構問題都可能成為 SEC 審查的焦點。

Confidential IPO filings let issuers resolve SEC comments before full public disclosure — standard for large tech debuts. OpenAI's unusual governance history (nonprofit → capped-profit → PBC) will face scrutiny. The company must also disclose its compute dependencies on Microsoft Azure, a potential risk factor investors will weigh.

影響觀察

OpenAI 上市若成功，將是 AI 產業的標誌性事件，也可能觸發 Anthropic、xAI 的跟進。然而，Anthropic 在同一週宣布估值超越 OpenAI，且實現盈利，形成強烈對比——這在機構投資人心中已埋下「誰才是最佳 AI 上市標的」的疑問。DeepSeek 永久降價帶來的邊際成本壓力，也將是 IPO 招股書中不可能迴避的風險章節。

A successful listing would be a watershed for the sector and likely accelerate Anthropic's and xAI's own paths to public markets. But Anthropic simultaneously reporting profitability at a higher valuation is a direct competitive narrative challenge. The S-1 will need to address long-term margin risk from open-weight models (DeepSeek) and the sustainability of the CapEx plan.

DeepSeek 宣布 V4-Pro 七五折永久生效，中國推論價格戰進入新階段 / DeepSeek Makes V4-Pro 75% Price Cut Permanent, Escalating the Inference Price War

來源 / Source: Engadget · 2026-05-23 · InfoWorld 分析
核心事實

DeepSeek 5 月 23 日宣布，原定 5 月 31 日到期的 V4-Pro 七五折促銷改為永久定價。新定價每百萬 token 輸入 $0.003625、輸出 $0.87，較原價下降 75%。V4-Pro 聲稱相較前代在超長 context 下僅需四分之一的計算量與十分之一的記憶體佔用，使效率增益得以直接反映於定價。

DeepSeek announced May 23 that its V4-Pro promotional 75% discount — previously set to expire May 31 — is now a permanent price point. New pricing: $0.003625–$0.87 per million tokens. The company attributes the cut to architectural gains (¼ compute, 1/10 memory vs. predecessor at long context), framing it as efficiency passthrough rather than subsidy.

背景脈絡

DeepSeek 自 2025 年初以 R1 系列震驚市場後，已持續以「低成本前沿模型」路線挑戰美國 AI 廠商。V4-Pro 延續此策略，以開源架構設計結合高效推論核心，在 1M context 場景下提供接近 GPT-4o/Claude Opus 等級的性能，而成本遠低於後者。永久降價進一步鞏固「中國推論即商品」的市場敘事。

DeepSeek has consistently used low-cost releases to put pressure on US frontier labs. V4-Pro, released in April, targeted the long-context inference segment with an open-weight design that has since been cloned by several Chinese and European providers. Making the cut permanent removes any hope of price normalization after the promotion ended.

影響觀察

此舉對 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的 API 毛利構成直接壓力，尤其在開發者市場——對成本敏感的新創若轉向 V4-Pro，兩家公司的 token 使用量成長將承壓。更深遠的影響是：這加強了「推論即商品」的敘事，使得大型 API 廠商必須在「差異化能力」（安全、整合生態、可靠性）上找到護城河，而非單純的模型性能。OpenAI IPO 招股書的風險章節預計將被追問此議題。

Direct margin pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic API revenue. Developers with cost elasticity have a clear migration path. The deeper structural concern: this validates the commodity-inference thesis, forcing frontier labs to prove durable differentiation through safety rails, ecosystem integrations, and enterprise reliability — none of which appear in benchmark tables.

來源 / Source: Anthropic Blog · 2026-05-28 · TechCrunch · VentureBeat
核心事實

Anthropic 同步 Series H 宣布推出 Claude Opus 4.8，Agentic coding 評分從 64.3% 升至 69.2%（+8%），多學科推理從 54.7% 升至 57.9%，知識工作分從 1753 升至 1890。Fast Mode（2.5x 速度）現在比前代便宜三倍。API 定價維持 $5/M input、$25/M output，適用 90% prompt caching 折扣及 50% batch 折扣。Claude Code 新增「Dynamic Workflows」功能，可應對超大規模長任務。

Opus 4.8 ships with concrete benchmark gains: agentic coding +8% (64.3→69.2%), multidisciplinary reasoning with tools +6%, and a new "Dynamic Workflows" feature in Claude Code for very large-scale tasks. Fast mode pricing drops 3× vs. Opus 4.7. Available on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise and on AWS, GCP, and Microsoft Foundry.

背景脈絡

Opus 4.8 是 Anthropic 在五個月內推出的第三個 Opus 主版本（4.6 → 4.7 → 4.8），顯示其「快速迭代」策略已完全複製 OpenAI 的節奏。值得注意的是，VentureBeat 的評測指出 4.8 在誠實性（honesty）和「不過度確信」行為上的改善，接近尚未公開的 Mythos 基準——這是首次有外部報導將 4.8 定位為 Mythos 的「安全公開替代品」。

Opus 4.8 is the third major Opus version in five months, matching OpenAI's pace. The honesty/calibration improvements are significant: early testers report Opus 4.8 is substantially less likely to make unsupported claims than 4.7, a shift aligned with Anthropic's Constitutional AI research rather than purely benchmark-driven training.

影響觀察

Dynamic Workflows 是最值得開發者關注的新功能：允許 Claude Code 自主分解超過原本 context 上限的任務，與 GitHub Copilot（已整合 Opus 4.8）直接競爭 Cursor、Windsurf 等 coding agent 市場。Fast Mode 降價三倍則大幅降低高頻 API 應用的邊際成本，有助於企業在 Agentic workflows 中大量部署 Claude。

Dynamic Workflows puts Opus 4.8 in direct competition with Cursor and Windsurf for long-horizon coding tasks. The 3× cheaper Fast Mode is especially relevant for high-frequency enterprise deployments. GitHub Copilot's day-one integration of Opus 4.8 is the most immediate distribution signal: developers already inside VS Code now get Opus 4.8 without switching tools.

Project Glasswing 首月發現逾萬個高危漏洞，Claude Mythos 的雙用性引發政府關注 / Project Glasswing: Claude Mythos Finds 10,000+ Critical Vulnerabilities in Month One

來源 / Source: Anthropic Research · 2026-05-26 · Help Net Security · The Hacker News
核心事實

Anthropic 發布 Project Glasswing 首次進展報告：在 30 天內，Claude Mythos Preview 與約 50 個合作組織識別出逾 10,000 個高危或嚴重（high/critical）漏洞，其中 6,202 個跨越 1,000+ 開源項目被確認，1,726 個驗證為真實漏洞，1,094 個構成嚴重安全風險。Cloudflare 識別 2,000 個 bug（400 個高/嚴重），Mozilla 在 Firefox 中修復 271 個漏洞（是前代 Claude 模型發現數量的 10 倍）。IBM 於 5 月 19 日加入聯盟。

In 30 days, Claude Mythos Preview + ~50 partners found 10,000+ high/critical vulns across 1,000+ open-source projects. Cloudflare: 2,000 bugs (400 high/critical). Mozilla Firefox: 271 fixes — 10× the yield of earlier Claude models. IBM joined on May 19. Anthropic has privately briefed US officials that uncontrolled release of Mythos could make large-scale cyberattacks "significantly more likely."

背景脈絡

Project Glasswing 於 4 月 7 日發布時，Anthropic 採取了前所未見的「封閉前沿模型」策略：Mythos Preview 僅透過審核合作夥伴（包括 AWS、Apple、Google、Microsoft、Cisco、NVIDIA 等）取得，而非公開於 Claude API。這是 AI 實驗室首次為單一模型設立如此嚴格的「know your customer」機制，背後的原因是 Mythos 被測試發現能夠自主發現並武器化零日漏洞。

Glasswing represents the first time a frontier lab has gated a major model behind a vetted, equity-free consortium rather than an API paywall. The rationale: Mythos can autonomously discover and weaponize zero-days, making unrestricted release a national security issue rather than just a safety one. The 45+ partners effectively serve as a supervised release channel.

影響觀察

10,000 個漏洞的數字令人印象深刻，但更重要的政策含義是：AI 模型已進入「必須按照武器標準管控」的時代。這為正在推進的伊利諾伊州 SB 315（要求年度第三方稽核）提供了現實依據，也預示未來的監管重點將不止於「偏見、歧視」等社會影響，而是擴展至「網路武器能力」。各大企業的安全團隊應盡速申請 Glasswing 合作。

The 10,000-vulnerability headline will anchor regulatory debates for months. The deeper implication: AI safety policy is bifurcating into "social harm" regulation (bias, deception) and "national security" regulation (cyber-capable models). Glasswing is Anthropic's attempt to define the latter framework before governments do. Enterprise security teams not yet in the consortium should apply immediately — the attack surface intelligence advantage is substantial.

For AI startups not locked into Azure or GCP, Trainium3 + Bedrock integration is now the most credible non-NVIDIA option.
