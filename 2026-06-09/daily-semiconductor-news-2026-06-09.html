Morgan Lewis · 2026-01 · 法律分析 ｜ US BIS Final Rule · Effective 2026-01-15
核心事實 Facts

美國商務部工業安全局（BIS）2026-01-15 生效最終規則：將特定高階運算半導體出口至中國及澳門的許可審查政策，從「預設拒絕」（Presumption of Denial）改為附嚴格條件的「逐案審查」（Case-by-Case Licensing）。同日白宮宣布對同等效能半導體加征 25% Section 232 關稅。
BIS final rule effective Jan 15, 2026: shifts advanced computing semiconductor export review for China/Macau from presumption of denial to case-by-case with strict conditions. White House simultaneously imposed 25% Section 232 tariffs on equivalent-performance chips.

Policy shift reflects late-Biden to Trump administration transition. Case-by-case review preserves some US company flexibility while maintaining effective controls on advanced AI chips to China. Global bifurcated chip ecosystem accelerates.

NVIDIA、AMD 等企業可重新申請部分受管制出口，但附嚴格使用條件，實際突破有限。中國內需晶片廠商（華為、寒武紀）因此獲得更長的保護窗口。（分析）
NVIDIA/AMD can re-apply for some controlled exports with strict end-use conditions; practical impact limited. China domestic chip makers (Huawei, Cambricon) gain extended protection windows. (Analysis)
