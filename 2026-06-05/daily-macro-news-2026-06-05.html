來源：OECD · 2026-06-04 · OECD June 2026 Outlook Summary
核心事實 / Facts
OECD 六月展望採雙情境架構：

基準假設：波斯灣石油生產自 2026 Q3 起逐步恢復。若衝突延長至 2027 下半年，全球通膨和下行壓力均顯著惡化。韓國獲 G20 最大升幅，預測調升 +0.9pp 至 2.6%。

OECD's June Economic Outlook uses a dual scenario. Baseline: global GDP 2.8% in 2026 (from 3.4% in 2025), contingent on Gulf oil recovery beginning Q3. Prolonged disruption scenario sees materially worse stagflationary outcomes.
背景脈絡 / Context

Since the February 2026 Iran war outbreak, Brent surged from ~$72 to over $120 at peak, now ranging $90–100. This supply-side shock combined with fiscal stimulus has placed major central banks in a stagflationary bind — justifying the unusual dual-scenario format.
影響觀察 / Implications
若基準情境成立，2026 年下半年全球利率路徑或出現分化：美聯儲偏鷹、ECB 繼續升息，而 BOJ 與部分亞太央行則在成長下行與通膨上行之間艱難取捨。延長衝突情境則可能引發新興市場資本外流與信用利差擴大。

Baseline realization would widen G10 rate divergence. Prolonged scenario risks EM capital outflows, wider credit spreads, and potential commodity demand destruction.

Energy inflation continues feeding into headline CPI. Most acute for eurozone (energy CPI weight) and Japan (import-dependent). A sustained break above $100 would further erode consumer confidence globally.
