Vatican Releases AI Encyclical on Protection of Humanity; Anthropic Co-Founder Joins Stage

來源 / Source: Multiple outlets · 2026-05-26 · imfounder.com
核心事實

今日（5月26日），教宗利奧十四世（Pope Leo XIV）在梵蒂岡正式發布以「AI 時代人類保護」為主題的通諭（Encyclical），同時成立涵蓋全球 14 億教徒的 AI 倫理委員會。Anthropic 共同創辦人 Chris Olah 受邀出席同台，為梵蒂岡在 AI 議題上尋求科技業對話的里程碑事件。

Today (May 26), Pope Leo XIV formally issued an encyclical on the protection of humanity in the AI era at the Vatican, simultaneously establishing an AI ethics committee representing 1.4 billion believers worldwide. Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah joined the stage, marking a landmark moment of dialogue between the Church and the AI industry.

背景脈絡

梵蒂岡長期關注科技倫理，但以正式通諭（最高層級教義文件）回應 AI 為史上首見。教宗利奧十四世（前名 Robert Prevost，2025年5月當選，為首位美國籍教宗）在上任初期即多次公開談及 AI 對人類尊嚴的威脅。Anthropic 的 Chris Olah 以「可解釋性研究」（Mechanistic Interpretability）著稱，是少數在學術與產業界均被視為 AI 安全良心的代表人物。

The Vatican has long engaged with technology ethics, but issuing a formal encyclical — the highest-level doctrinal document — in response to AI is unprecedented. Pope Leo XIV (formerly Robert Prevost, elected May 2025, the first American pope) had repeatedly raised concerns about AI's threat to human dignity early in his papacy. Chris Olah, known for pioneering mechanistic interpretability research, is one of the few figures viewed as an AI safety conscience by both academia and industry.

影響觀察

梵蒂岡的通諭雖無法律效力，但對全球天主教社群的道德影響力不容低估，將為正在進行中的 EU AI Act 最終合規期（8月2日）與聯合國 AI 治理框架談判增添道德維度。對 Anthropic 而言，此舉強化了其「負責任 AI」的品牌定位，在 OpenAI 積極推動 IPO、商業化擴張的背景下，提供了差異化的公眾形象。

While the encyclical carries no legal force, its moral authority over the global Catholic community is significant. It will add an ethical dimension to ongoing EU AI Act compliance negotiations (August 2 deadline) and UN AI governance talks. For Anthropic, the appearance reinforces its "responsible AI" brand positioning — a meaningful differentiator as OpenAI aggressively pursues its IPO and commercial scale-up.

OpenAI confidentially filed its S-1 with the SEC on May 22, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the underwriting. The target valuation ranges from $852 billion to over $1 trillion, with a potential Q4 2026 public listing. The company generates ~$2B/month in revenue (annualized $25B run rate), but was losing $1.22 for every $1 of revenue in Q1 2026.

背景脈絡

OpenAI 已歷經多年組織重組——從非營利轉型為公益公司（PBC），並在 2026 年初完成 1,220 億美元的史上最大單輪融資。IPO 文件將首次揭露訓練成本、資料中心支出與企業合約結構等財務細節。根據 S-1 封存規定，文件在公開路演前 15 天才解密，預計 9 月為最早可能的掛牌窗口。

OpenAI has undergone years of restructuring — converting from a nonprofit to a Public Benefit Corporation — and closed the largest single venture round in history ($122B) in early 2026. The S-1 will for the first time disclose training costs, data center capex, and enterprise contract structures. Under SEC confidential filing rules, the document stays sealed until ~15 days before the public roadshow, with September as the earliest realistic window.

影響觀察

此次 IPO 將是科技史上規模最大的上市案之一。一旦成功，將對整個 AI 生態系的估值基準產生重定錨效應：Anthropic、xAI 等私人公司的估值將面臨重新審視。然而，持續虧損的體質與全球算力短缺（矽谷 Q1 出現算力荒）也讓市場對盈利路徑心存疑慮。投資人將緊盯企業客戶佔比（已超過 40%）與 API token 消耗量（每分鐘 150 億）兩項指標。

This would be one of the largest tech IPOs in history. A successful listing would reset valuation anchors across the AI ecosystem — privately held Anthropic and xAI would face immediate re-pricing scrutiny. However, persistent losses and the Q1 Silicon Valley compute shortage raise questions about the path to profitability. Investors will watch enterprise revenue share (already above 40%) and API token throughput (15B tokens/minute) as proxy metrics for monetization quality.

核心事實

Axios 與 Gallup 調查顯示，超過 70% 的美國人認為 AI 發展速度太快（共和黨 68%、民主黨 77%），14–29 歲年輕人中僅 18% 對 AI 感到樂觀。2026 年 Q1 美國資料中心取消案例創歷史新高，Oregon 州已通過一年期資料中心減稅暫停令；Seattle、Portland 均出現地方性抗議運動。

Q1 2026 saw a record number of US data center cancellations; Oregon passed a one-year moratorium on data-center tax breaks; Seattle and Portland have seen local protest movements.

背景脈絡

AI 帶動的電力需求暴增（資料中心平均用電量等同一座中型城市）、就業焦慮（69% 的企業主管計劃裁員以換 AI）以及財富集中效應，正共同催生廣泛的社會反彈。年輕世代的不滿尤為值得關注——這批人是最熟悉 AI 工具的使用者，卻也是最擔心 AI 搶奪職涯機會的族群。

AI-driven power demand surges (data centers consuming city-scale electricity), job anxiety (69% of executives planning AI-related layoffs), and wealth concentration are collectively fueling broad social pushback. Gen Z's disillusionment is particularly notable — this cohort is the most AI-literate yet most worried about career displacement.

影響觀察

此一民意趨勢正轉化為真實的政治與商業風險：各州資料中心選址阻力上升，將推高 AI 基礎設施成本並延緩算力擴張。69% 企業計劃 AI 裁員的數據若兌現，可能引爆勞工團體針對科技業的更大規模組織動員，進而影響 2026 年期中選舉議程。對投資人而言，Axios 明確將此列為影響 AI 股票的「真實業務風險」。

This sentiment is converting into real political and business risk: rising data center siting resistance will increase AI infrastructure costs and slow compute expansion. If 69% of executives follow through on AI layoffs, larger-scale labor mobilization targeting tech firms could emerge, influencing the 2026 midterm agenda. Axios has explicitly classified this trend as a "real business risk" for AI stocks.

Google's deliberate choice to lead with the Flash (lighter, faster) variant rather than the Pro flagship is a clear strategic signal: in edge deployments and agentic scenarios (long-horizon tasks, high-frequency API calls), speed and cost-efficiency matter more than peak benchmark scores. Gemini 3.5 Flash pricing will directly pressure Anthropic Claude Haiku 4 and OpenAI GPT-4o Mini — these three compete head-to-head in enterprise "high-volume inference" use cases. Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview: Autonomously Finds & Exploits Unpatched Vulnerabilities; Access Restricted to 40 Orgs

來源 / Source: Anthropic Red Team Blog · Turing Institute CETAS · 2026-04-07 onwards · red.anthropic.com · cetas.turing.ac.uk
核心事實

Anthropic 於 4月7日發布 Claude Mythos Preview——一個通用語言模型，但在資安任務上展現出令人警惕的能力：能識別並利用主流作業系統與瀏覽器中超過 99% 尚未修補的零日漏洞（Zero-day），首次嘗試即成功開發可用漏洞利用程式的比率超過 83%。Anthropic 未將此模型公開發布，僅授權 40 個關鍵軟體組織進行監控存取，同步啟動「Project Glasswing」負責任揭露計畫。

Anthropic released Claude Mythos Preview on April 7 — a general-purpose model that exhibits alarming cybersecurity capabilities: it can identify and exploit unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities in major OSes and browsers (99%+ not yet patched across leading platforms), with over 83% first-attempt exploit success. Anthropic has withheld public release, granting monitored access to 40 critical software organizations and launching "Project Glasswing" for responsible disclosure.

背景脈絡

Anthropic 強調，Mythos 的資安能力並非刻意訓練的產物，而是通用推理與軟體工程能力提升的「湧現」結果（Emergent capability）。這是 AI 安全領域長期警告「能力誤對齊」（Capability misalignment）的現實版教材：一個設計用途良善的模型，因為能力太強而產生了設計者未預期的高風險能力。

Anthropic emphasizes that Mythos's cyber capabilities were not explicitly trained — they are an emergent consequence of general improvements in reasoning and software engineering. This is a real-world textbook case of the "capability misalignment" that AI safety researchers have long warned about: a well-intentioned model develops high-risk capabilities its designers did not anticipate.

影響觀察

Mythos 案例將成為監管機構制定「前部署評估義務」（Pre-deployment evaluation mandates）的核心論據，美國 CAISI 與 EU AI Act 均可能以此為由加速強制安全評估的立法進程。對其他 AI 實驗室而言，這也是一個警訊：下一代模型可能同樣會浮現未被預期的高風險能力，現有的安全測試框架是否足夠？

The Mythos case will become a central argument for regulators pushing "pre-deployment evaluation mandates." Both US CAISI and the EU AI Act may cite this as justification for accelerating mandatory safety evaluation legislation. For other AI labs, it is a warning: next-generation models may similarly surface unanticipated high-risk capabilities — are current safety testing frameworks sufficient?
