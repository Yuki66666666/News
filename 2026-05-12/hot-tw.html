為什麼今天熱 / Why it's hot today

聯發科自4月法說會（4/30）宣布 AI ASIC 年度營收目標從10億美元上修至20億美元後，連拉多根漲停，股價從低點波段狂飆147%，市值一度破5.8兆元，成為史上市值最大的台股處置股。5月12日雖仍在處置期（每5分鐘撮合一次），但仍是全市場成交最熱個股之一，多空激烈拉鋸。PTT 股板大量討論「處置出關後劇本」，分析師普遍看多至4字頭以上。

MediaTek surged 147% from its April lows after its Q1 earnings call (April 30) revealed AI ASIC revenue target doubled from $1B to $2B for 2026, with 2027 target raised to $7–12B. The stock was placed under disposal trading (every-5-min auction) starting May 7 — making it the largest-cap stock ever subjected to disposal rules on TWSE. Despite restrictions, it remains the hottest single-name in the market.
