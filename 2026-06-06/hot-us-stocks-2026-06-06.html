$AVGO — Broadcom Inc. / 博通
NASDAQ  |  半導體 AI 網路晶片  |  大跌 ▼13%+主角
★★★★★
Close: ▼13%+  |  Vol: 極高（$40B 美元量級）  |  市值蒸發 ~$800 億美元
為什麼今天熱 / Why It's Hot

Broadcom 於 6 月 3 日盤後公布 Q2 FY2026 財報，每股盈餘 $2.44（超預期 $2.32），但 AI 網路晶片（XPU + ethernet ASIC）季營收僅 40.1 億美元，遠低於市場共識的 48 億美元（miss 約 14%）。CEO Hock Tan 說明：兩家匿名超大型雲端客戶的客製 AI 晶片（XPU）量產爬坡比預期慢。這句話引爆整個 AI 晶片板塊的「估值修正」連鎖反應，Broadcom 在 6 月 4–5 日連跌兩天，累計跌幅逾 13%，並拖垮 Nvidia、AMD、Marvell、Micron、ARM、Intel 全面崩跌。

Broadcom's Q2 FY2026 report beat EPS ($2.44 vs $2.32 est.) but badly missed AI networking revenue ($4.1B vs $4.8B consensus — a 14% miss on the metric that matters most). CEO Hock Tan cited two unnamed hyperscaler clients with slower-than-expected XPU custom chip ramps. The miss ignited a sector-wide AI chip selloff across June 4–5, dragging the SOXX down 10.4% — its worst loss since March 2020. Broadcom itself shed ~$80B in market cap in two sessions.

Options & Flow

Put activity surged sharply as options traders scrambled to hedge or bet on further downside. IV spiked materially. The earnings miss on the most closely watched metric (AI networking revenue) drew the largest options flow of the week across semiconductors.

Retail Buzz

WSB 及 r/stocks 大量帖子討論 AVGO 崩跌是否預示 AI 泡沫破滅第一槍，或只是訂單時間點問題。主流論點分成兩派：「AI capex 延遲不等於取消」vs「AI 升溫故事被過度 price in」。

Reddit and X saw intense debate: bulls argued XPU delays are timing issues (not cancellations), bears warned AI chip estimates need to be reset lower across the board. Key quote: "AVGO guided AI up 20% YoY but that means nothing if the market priced in 40%"

後續觀察 / What to Watch Next

觀察 Nvidia FY27 Q1 財報（後續）、Microsoft/Google/Amazon 的 Capex guidance 是否下修；AVGO 股價能否守住關鍵技術支撐。分析師普遍下調 AI networking 短期預估，但長期 thesis 維持。

Micron 本身並無任何負面消息，但因 AVGO 財報暴露了 AI 晶片需求放緩疑慮，市場擔心 AI 相關 HBM（High Bandwidth Memory）需求也會受衝擊，對 Micron 造成嚴重連鎖賣壓。MU 單日重挫 12.32%，以 $40 億美元的美元成交量高居 S&P 500 最大成交量股票，成為全市場最熱的交易標的之一。下一個重大催化劑是 6 月 24 日的 Q3 FY2026 財報，華爾街預估 EPS $19.29、營收 $338.8 億美元。

MU had no company-specific bad news, but the AVGO-induced fear that AI capex spending may be decelerating hammered HBM memory demand expectations. Micron, as the primary supplier of HBM3E to hyperscalers, bore the brunt. It led the S&P 500 lower with over $40B in dollar volume — an extraordinary trading day. Next catalyst: Q3 FY2026 earnings June 24 (consensus: EPS $19.29, Rev $33.88B).

Options & Flow

Large put flows during the session; IV spiked. With Q3 earnings ~18 days away, some options activity reflects pre-earnings hedging and directional bets on whether the HBM narrative remains intact post-AVGO.

Retail Buzz

WSB 上大量「MU 抄底」討論，但也有「HBM 過熱、估值修正才剛開始」的聲音。AI 記憶體多頭長期 thesis 尚未崩潰，但短期動能急速惡化。

WSB and r/investing saw heavy "dip buy vs. avoid" debate. Long-term HBM bulls argue demand is structural; bears cite AVGO's XPU slowdown as a leading indicator for memory pull-forward. Quote: "MU at this price is a gift if HBM demand holds"

後續觀察 / What to Watch Next

6 月 24 日財報是最大觀察點。若 Micron 財報 HBM 出貨量如期，將是重要反彈催化劑；若出現保守 guidance，股價可能再度承壓。注意 $850 技術支撐能否守住。

為什麼今天熱 / Why It's Hot

本週最具戲劇性的「雲霄飛車股」。6 月 2 日，Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang 在公開場合稱 Marvell 為「下一個兆美元公司」，單日暴漲 32.52%，創公司史上最大單日漲幅。6 月 3 日盤前再漲 10%。然而 AVGO 財報暴露 AI 網路晶片需求疑慮後，Marvell 作為最直接的 AI ASIC/連接晶片同業，成為本輪跌幅最重的標的之一，兩日合計重挫約 16%，幾乎吐回一半漲幅。

The week's most dramatic round-trip. Jensen Huang's "next trillion-dollar company" endorsement on June 2 sent MRVL up 32.52% in a single session — its all-time best day. The stock had surged on strong Q1 FY27 results (data center rev +76% to $1.83B) and a new long-term revenue target of $16.5B for FY2028. But AVGO's AI networking miss reframed Marvell's NVLink Fusion and photonics story as potentially facing the same hyperscaler slowdown. MRVL pulled back ~16% over June 4–5, giving back nearly half the Jensen pop.

Options & Flow

After days of extreme bullish call flow following the Huang endorsement, the C/P ratio reversed sharply as the AVGO ripple hit. IV remains extremely elevated — the stock is a prime vehicle for leveraged bets in both directions.

Retail Buzz

X 和 Reddit 上「買 MRVL 還是等？」討論最熱。Nvidia 持有 MRVL 約 20 億美元股份，且 NVLink Fusion 合作為真實合作，多頭認為基本面敘事未變。

X ($MRVL) saw intense dip-buying discussion. Bulls note Nvidia owns $2B stake and the NVLink partnership is concrete. Bears: "If AVGO hyperscalers slow XPU ramps, MRVL ASIC orders face the same risk." Quote: "Jensen called it trillion dollar but the customers still control the timing"

後續觀察 / What to Watch Next

關注 Nvidia 下一季財報中是否提及 Marvell 合作進度、大型雲端廠 Capex guidance 修正幅度。MRVL 是否能維持 $16.5B FY2028 目標是關鍵。Gamma 擠壓風險仍存（float 的 large options OI 後遺症）。

$NYXH — Nyxoah SA / 奈克索
NASDAQ  |  醫療器材 / 睡眠呼吸中止症  |  崩跌 ▼49.7%
★★★

為什麼今天熱 / Why It's Hot

Nyxoah SA 是比利時醫療器材公司，其 Genio 系統為 FDA 核准的舌下神經刺激器，用於治療中重度阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止症（OSA），僅需單一切口植入。6 月 4 日宣布以 $1.72 每股（約 EUR 1.48）定價 5,523 萬股的折讓配售，較前一日收盤價打了近五折，總募資 $9,500 萬美元，將用於美國商業化推廣。大折讓配售導致原有股東稀釋嚴重，股價單日腰斬近五成。

Nyxoah priced a $95M underwritten public offering at $1.72 per share — a deep discount representing roughly a 50% cut from the prior close — issuing 55.2M shares to fund U.S. commercialization of its Genio hypoglossal neurostimulation OSA device. Heavily dilutive secondary offerings at steep discounts almost always trigger immediate sharp selloffs as arbitrageurs short against the deal. A -49.7% single-day drop is exactly the market's reaction to such a pricing.
