為什麼今天熱 / Why it's hot today

SpaceX 在 6 月 12 日（美東時間）以 $135 完成史上最大 IPO，募資 $750 億，首日收漲 19%。 這是美股史上最大規模 IPO，遠超 Aramco 等前例，市值站上 $2.1 兆，躋身美國第六大上市公司。 6 月 13 日 MSCI 提前納入生效，估計全球被動基金須買入 $15–20 兆美元（以 SPCX 4% 流通股計，供給極度緊縮）， 散戶在 Robinhood、WallStreetBets 瘋狂搶進，Cashtag 提及量爆炸。

SpaceX priced its historic $75B IPO at $135 on June 12, opened at $150, hit $176.52 intraday before closing at $161.11 — a 19% pop. The debut is the largest IPO ever, eclipsing Saudi Aramco. On June 13, MSCI early-inclusion kicked in, with an estimated $15–20B in passive-fund inflows forced into just 4% of outstanding shares (tight float → structural buy pressure). Elon Musk celebrated by having SpaceX launch a Florida rocket ~1 hour before market open.
