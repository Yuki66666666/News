生態系統能「失靈」嗎？ / Can Ecosystems Malfunction?
Aeon · John Drake (U. of Georgia) · 2026-05-25 · 5,400 words
為什麼值得讀 / Why it stands out
這是一篇哲學與生態學罕見交匯的長文。在「自然界正在崩潰」的敘事鋪天蓋地的今天，作者逆向追問：「崩潰」這個詞到底在說什麼？生態系統有功能可以「失靈」嗎？論點嚴密、邏輯清晰，而且最終指向更大的倫理責任問題。

In an era saturated with "nature is breaking down" rhetoric, a rare piece that turns the lens on the concept itself: can forests or reefs truly "malfunction," or are we projecting human purposes onto systems that have none?
摘要 / Summary
Drake 梳理兩種功能理論——「因果角色理論」（Cummins）與「選擇效應理論」（Millikan/Neander）——並指出前者無法界定失靈標準，後者需要演化選擇史，而生態系統恰恰不是被演化「設計」的整體。生態系統的「功能」語言是 20 世紀中葉保育科學為了政策說服力而刻意引入的，代價是讓科學語言混入了人類中心主義的規範判斷。

Drake traces how "ecosystem function" entered ecology not from first principles but from policy rhetoric — the 1993 volume Biodiversity and Ecosystem Function borrowed the metaphors of cars and airplanes to make biodiversity loss viscerally legible to policymakers. But the language smuggles in teleology: the car exists for transport; the Amazon does not exist for storing carbon. Ecosystems can only "malfunction" relative to human-defined goals — an honest acknowledgment that actually strengthens the case for conservation, since it forces us to own our values rather than hiding them behind pseudo-scientific neutrality.
"Ecosystems cannot malfunction on their own. They may change, reorganise or even collapse. But these should be understood as natural processes, not failures."
引發的問題 / Questions it opens
如果「生態失靈」本質上是人類價值的投射，那麼 ESG 報告中的「生態系統服務」指標究竟在衡量什麼？這場哲學釐清，會讓自然資本會計更誠實，還是更麻煩？

Laurent Le Brun · 前 Google 開發工具 Tech Lead (2011–2024) · 2026-05-09 · HN 351 pts

A rare insider account of how one of the world's largest engineering organizations navigated a 15-year IDE consolidation — from editor chaos to 80% adoption of a single tool — and why standardization created leverage that only became visible at scale.
摘要 / Summary

"In the end, standard tooling creates leverage."

拒絕死去的泥土 / The Dirt That Refused to Die
Quanta Magazine · Siddhant Pusdekar · 2026-06-01 · HN 268 pts
為什麼值得讀 / Why it stands out
一個為期六年、幾乎沒人相信的實驗，最終指向生命起源的核心問題。Quanta 寫科學從不灌水，這篇把一個「土壤怎麼還在呼吸」的怪問題，拉升到「代謝先於生命」的演化哲學高度，引人入勝。

A six-year experiment in sterilized soil that kept exhaling CO₂ — and the slow, stubborn scientific detective work that turned a lab anomaly into a potential window on how life began.
摘要 / Summary
法國國家農業研究院的 Sébastien Fontaine 想測量無生命土壤的碳排放基線。他用伽馬射線把土壤徹底滅菌，然後等 CO₂ 數值降為零。但它就是不降——六年後依然持續排放。他的燃料電池實驗顯示，滅菌土壤中存在類似克雷伯斯循環（Krebs cycle）的電子流動。2025 年預印本在六個月大的無菌土壤中找到克雷伯斯循環的四種中間產物。結論指向：生命代謝的部分化學反應可能在無生命環境下自發發生，甚至早於生命本身存在。

After 15 years and gamma-ray sterilization cycles intense enough to leave cells DNA-free, Fontaine's soil kept metabolizing. His team found Krebs-cycle intermediates forming in sterile soil, and built a fuel cell to measure electron flow. The debate now is whether residual enzymes from dead cells are responsible (they degrade exponentially; six years seems too long) or whether soil minerals genuinely catalyze these reactions — supporting the "metabolism-first" theory of life's origin.
"It's the chemistry of geology." — Joseph Moran, University of Ottawa

壽命遺傳率重新定義後約 50%——也就是說，重新定義了壽命 / Heritability of Human Lifespan is ~50% When Heritability is Redefined
Dynomight · dynomight · 2026-02-05 · HN 92 pts (本週重新浮現)
為什麼值得讀 / Why it stands out
這篇文章用一個關於壽命遺傳率的 Science 論文作為解剖對象，教的卻是如何閱讀任何一篇科學論文：注意它的假設、它選擇的「世界」、它標題暗示的比實際論點大得多的主張。寫得極好，諷刺不失公允。

Uses a high-profile Science paper on lifespan heritability as a dissection table to teach statistical reading: the difference between "heritability in our world" and "heritability in a hypothetical world with no accidents, murder, or drug overdoses" is not a finding — it's a definitional choice.
摘要 / Summary
傳統雙胞胎研究估計人類壽命遺傳率為 23-35%。這篇 Science 論文建立模擬器，在「沒有非老化死因」的假想世界中重跑計算，得出約 46-57%——但這不是說壽命「其實比我們以為的更遺傳」，而是說「在一個沒有車禍、謀殺、藥物過量的世界裡，遺傳率比較高」。作者指出，論文所謂「現代條件下」的遺傳率，從其自己的圖表讀出來其實更接近 35-45%，而非標題宣稱的 50%。核心教訓：遺傳率是一個比值，不是什麼柏拉圖本質，它永遠隨社會環境而改變。

The paper's actual finding: in a simulator where extrinsic mortality (accidents, violence) is zeroed out, heritability rises. That's tautologically true of any trait. What's genuinely useful: this gives a tool to estimate how heritability of lifespan has changed as modern medicine reduced non-aging deaths. What's misleading: calling the result "50%" when the authors' own figures show ~35–45% at modern extrinsic mortality levels.
「遺傳率是一個比值。你不應該試著讓它變成一個非比值，因為非比值的東西不可能是比值。」

Not campus news — a primary-source document of how AI is dismantling institutional trust frameworks built over a century. 摘要 / Summary
普林斯頓 1893 年的榮譽制度起源於學生請願要求撤除監考——以學生自我負責取代外部監控。133 年後，AI 作弊工具讓「用小設備作弊的外觀與不作弊完全相同」，同學間相互舉報的意願也降至接近零（只有 0.4% 的大四生曾舉報過他人）。新政策從 7 月 1 日起，教師須在考場中作為「見證人」，但仍不干預——榮譽制度的框架名義上保留，現實上卻已根本改變。

"I think it's a shame, but it's necessary." — Jill Dolan, former Dean of the College

Aeon / John Drake — 如果生態系統沒有內在功能，環保運動的語言框架需要重建嗎？（延伸思考）
延伸自今日編輯精選 · Aeon · 2026-05-25 · 原文連結
為什麼值得讀 / Why it stands out
Drake 的文章在今日的論點層面值得單獨放在 Track B：作為一位生態學者，他選擇拆解自己領域的核心語言，而非捍衛它。這是科學社群少見的誠實——不是向外批評，而是向內質問。Peter Calow（Functional Ecology 期刊創辦人）承認，採用「functional」這個詞純粹是因為「討論討論累了，就這樣定了」。

Drake's article is itself a form of intellectual courage — an ecologist dismantling the rhetorical scaffolding his own field has relied on for conservation advocacy, because intellectual honesty matters more than winning the policy argument.
核心觀點 / Core Insight
科學界長期用「功能性語言」讓生態保護對政策制定者更有說服力，但代價是悄悄地把人類中心主義的價值判斷，偽裝成科學中立的事實陳述。Drake 的提案：直接承認「我們保護生態，是因為我們選擇保護它，而不是因為自然界有某種本質義務被保護」——這實際上是更強的道德論點，因為它要求人類負起真正的道德責任，而非躲在「自然法則」後面。
