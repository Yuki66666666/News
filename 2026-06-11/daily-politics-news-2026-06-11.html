影響觀察 Implications (分析) 公約的實際執行效力仍受質疑，強制性分擔機制能否獲匈牙利、波蘭等強烈反對移民政策的成員國配合，是初期最大挑戰。Enforcement credibility is the key test: whether Hungary and Poland will meaningfully comply with mandatory solidarity is the central question for the Pact's first year.
