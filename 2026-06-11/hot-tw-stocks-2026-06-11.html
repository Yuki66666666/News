Amid the carnage, passive components hit limit-up on AI demand tailwinds, and construction/real estate stocks surged after CBC Governor Yang Jin-long signaled no further credit-tightening measures.

為什麼今天熱 / Why It's Hot

國巨 5 月自結合併營收 150.58 億元，月增 7.3%，連續 3 個月刷新單月歷史新高。前 5 月累計合併營收 672.63 億元，年增 27.4%。AI 伺服器與 CoWoS 封裝對高規格 MLCC（Multi-Layer Ceramic Capacitors）需求大爆發，國巨作為全球前三大被動元件廠，訂單能見度延伸至 2026Q3。開盤即拉上漲停 908 元（前一交易日 6/9 出關後已大漲，6/10 再度開高），創歷史新天價。然而因本輪漲幅過快（820→908，+10.7%），盤中遭大量獲利賣壓壓回，最終收盤 819 元，呈上影線收跌。

Yageo reported record May revenue (NT$15.06B, +7.3% MoM, +27.4% YoY), driven by AI server MLCC demand explosion. Stock gapped to the 10% limit-up price (NT$908) at open on June 10, but intense profit-taking reversed it to close at NT$819. The stock was recently under TWSE disposition (May 26–June 8), which expired — releasing pent-up energy.
