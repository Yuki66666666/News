Anthropic 發布 Claude Fable 5 與 Mythos 5，首次開放 Mythos 等級能力 Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 — Mythos-Class Capabilities Now Broadly Available

Anthropic Newsroom · 2026-06-09 · anthropic.com

核心事實
Anthropic 於 6 月 9 日正式推出 Claude Fable 5 及 Claude Mythos 5。Fable 5 是首個對大眾開放的 Mythos 等級模型，定價為每百萬輸入 token $10、輸出 $50，不到 Claude Mythos Preview 的一半。Stripe 測試中，Fable 5 在一天內完成了一個五千萬行 Ruby 代碼庫的大規模遷移，估計人工需要超過兩個月。Mythos 5 與 Fable 5 底層相同，但針對已通過審查的網路安全組織（Project Glasswing 夥伴）解除了部分安全限制，具備目前世界最強的網路安全能力。

Anthropic officially launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9. Fable 5 is the first Mythos-class model made generally available, priced at $10/M input tokens and $50/M output — under half the cost of Mythos Preview. In enterprise testing, Stripe reported Fable 5 completed a codebase-wide migration of a 50-million-line Ruby codebase in a single day that would have taken a full team over two months. Mythos 5 shares the same underlying model but has cybersecurity safeguards lifted for vetted Project Glasswing partners.

背景脈絡
Mythos 等級是 Anthropic 在能力層級上位於 Opus 之上的全新分類，最早以受限版本 Claude Mythos Preview 的形式於今年四月透過 Project Glasswing 計畫發布，當時僅限美國政府合作的網路防禦夥伴。Anthropic 在此之前就已公開表示，計畫在安全防護足夠成熟後向大眾開放 Mythos 等級，此次發布兌現了這個承諾。模型新增「安全分類器」機制，當偵測到網路安全、生物化學相關或模型蒸餾請求時，會自動降級由 Opus 4.8 回應，觸發率低於 5%。

The Mythos tier is Anthropic's new capability level above Opus, first debuted as the restricted Claude Mythos Preview in April via Project Glasswing, available only to US government-vetted cyberdefenders. Anthropic had publicly committed to broader access once safeguards matured. The new safety classifier mechanism automatically falls back to Opus 4.8 for cybersecurity, bio/chemistry, and distillation queries, triggering in under 5% of sessions.

影響觀察
這是 2026 年迄今最重要的單一模型發布事件。Fable 5 在多個關鍵基準上（包括 FrontierCode、Hebbia Finance Benchmark）奪得第一，直接向 OpenAI GPT-5.5 施壓。藥物設計與基因組學自主研究的展示，標誌著 AI 在科學研究的實際應用進入新階段——Mythos 5 在無人類干預下完成的蛋白設計任務匹配或超越專業科學家。訂閱制用戶在 6 月 22 日後需要購買使用積分才能繼續用 Fable 5，這顯示 Anthropic 正在探索超出平訂閱的新收費模式。

This is the most significant single model launch of 2026 to date. Fable 5 tops multiple key benchmarks (FrontierCode, Hebbia Finance) and directly pressures OpenAI's GPT-5.5. The drug design and autonomous genomics research demonstrations signal a qualitative shift in AI's role in scientific research. The decision to remove Fable 5 from subscription plans after June 22 (moving to usage credits) signals Anthropic testing premium monetization models beyond flat subscriptions.

歐盟 AI 法案 8 月全面生效；科羅拉多州 AI 法在最後關頭大幅修訂 EU AI Act Full Enforcement Arrives August 2026; Colorado AI Law Gutted at the Last Minute

Multiple sources · 2026-05 to 06 · artificialintelligenceact.eu | Law & the Workplace

核心事實
歐盟《人工智慧法》（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689）將於 2026 年 8 月 2 日全面適用，屆時通用人工智慧（GPAI）模型的完整規範進入執法階段。同時，歐盟委員會原計畫在 6 月完成的 AI 生成內容標記與辨識行為準則已接近定案。在美國，科羅拉多州 SB24-205（原定 6 月 30 日生效）在距離生效不到兩個月時，州長簽署重大修訂法案（S.B. 26-189），大幅削減企業合規義務，生效日期推遲至 2027 年 1 月 1 日。

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) enters full enforcement on August 2, 2026, bringing complete GPAI model obligations into effect. The EU Commission's Code of Practice for AI-generated content labeling is also near finalization after a June deadline. In the US, Colorado's SB24-205 (originally effective June 30) was substantially amended by S.B. 26-189 signed by the governor less than two months before its original effective date, significantly reducing employer compliance burdens and pushing the effective date to January 1, 2027.

背景脈絡
歐盟 AI 法案自 2024 年 8 月生效以來，已分階段適用：禁止的 AI 應用（如實時生物辨識監控）最早生效，GPAI 規範次之，高風險 AI 的完整義務最後。8 月的全面生效是迄今執法強度最高的時間點。科羅拉多州的法案修訂則折射出美國各州在 AI 立法上面對產業遊說的普遍脆弱性——即便是相對有限的規範，也難逃最後時刻的侵蝕。

The EU AI Act has applied in phases since August 2024: prohibited AI practices first, GPAI rules next, full high-risk obligations last. August 2026 marks peak enforcement intensity. Colorado's last-minute amendment reflects a broader US pattern of state AI legislation facing effective lobbying — even moderate requirements proved vulnerable to last-minute erosion.

影響觀察
歐盟與美國之間的監管分歧正在加深：歐盟走向更嚴格的規則型框架，聯邦政府走向親創新的寬鬆路線，各州則搖擺不定。對跨國 AI 公司而言，這意味著合規成本將持續上升，特別是需要針對不同市場維護不同版本的模型行為（如 Siri AI 在歐盟 iOS 上的缺席即是現實案例）。

The EU–US regulatory divergence is deepening: the EU moves toward rule-based strictness, the federal executive branch toward pro-innovation permissiveness, and states oscillate. For cross-border AI companies, compliance costs will keep rising — requiring different model behaviors for different markets, as Siri AI's EU iOS absence already demonstrates in practice.
