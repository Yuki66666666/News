Qwen3.7-Max-Preview Tops Chinese LLM Rankings
Qwen3.7-Max-Preview 發布：中國模型首次衝上 LM Arena 文字與視覺雙榜前列

Alibaba released Qwen3.7-Max-Preview and Qwen3.7-Plus-Preview on May 18–20, 2026, coinciding with the Alibaba Cloud Summit in Hangzhou. The Max variant is designed for deep reasoning, coding, and math; the Plus variant leans into multimodal and vision tasks.

Benchmark highlights: Qwen3.7-Max-Preview sits at LM Arena overall rank #13 (Elo ~1,475), ranks #7 in Math and #4 in coding out of 117 evaluated models (avg score 92.2). Its context window stretches to 1M tokens. Alibaba claims the model can sustain 35-hour autonomous agentic runs and chain 1,000+ tool calls per session — figures awaiting third-party verification.

Technical note: Weights have not yet been released publicly; preview API access is available via Alibaba Cloud. The MoE architecture activates a fraction of parameters per token, delivering competitive throughput at lower inference cost.
阿里巴巴於 5 月 18–20 日趁雲棲大會發布 Qwen3.7-Max-Preview。該模型在 LM Arena 上以 Elo ~1,475 排全球第 13，數學榜第 7、程式碼榜第 4（117 模型中），支援 100 萬 Token 上下文。Plus 版偏多模態視覺；Max 版主攻深度推理與數學。官方聲稱可連續執行 35 小時 agentic 任務，目前仍待第三方驗證。

DeepMind Publishes Cognitive Framework for Measuring AGI Progress
DeepMind 發布 AGI 進度認知分類框架，並開放 $20 萬 Kaggle 評估挑戰賽

Google DeepMind released "Measuring Progress Toward AGI: A Cognitive Taxonomy" (published May 17, 2026), a paper that provides a structured, cognitive-science-grounded framework for systematically evaluating how close current AI systems are to AGI across multiple cognitive dimensions.

Alongside the paper, DeepMind announced a Kaggle hackathon with a $200,000 prize pool inviting participants to design novel evaluations for key cognitive abilities identified in the taxonomy — effectively crowdsourcing benchmark design for frontier models.

Why it matters: The lack of rigorous, agreed-upon AGI measurement frameworks has been a persistent critique of AGI timelines discourse. This paper is one of the first from a frontier lab to propose a systematic cognitive scaffold rather than relying purely on task-specific benchmarks.
DeepMind 5 月 17 日發表《邁向 AGI 進度量測：認知分類框架》，以認知科學為基礎提出多維度評估體系，同步辦理 Kaggle 競賽（獎金 $20 萬美元），公開徵集新穎的 AGI 評估方法。這是頂尖實驗室首次以系統性認知分類學取代單一任務基準來衡量 AGI 距離。

Research · Energy Efficiency
Neurosymbolic Hybrid Cuts AI Energy Use 100× While Improving Accuracy
神經符號混合架構實現百倍能耗降低，準確率同步提升

A newly published study demonstrates a radically more efficient AI approach that combines neural networks with symbolic reasoning, slashing energy consumption by up to 100× relative to pure deep learning baselines — while simultaneously improving accuracy on several benchmark tasks.

Separately, Google's TurboQuant algorithm (presented at ICLR 2026) reduces the memory overhead of the KV cache — one of the largest bottlenecks in long-context LLM inference — enabling longer effective context and lower serving cost at production scale.

Technical context: KV cache memory scales linearly with sequence length and batch size, making it the dominant memory cost in long-context inference. TurboQuant applies learned quantization to KV entries, compressing them without significant quality degradation.
一項新研究結合神經網路與符號推理，能耗較純深度學習降低最高 100 倍，準確率反而提升。Google 則在 ICLR 2026 展示 TurboQuant 演算法，對 KV Cache 施以學習型量化壓縮，大幅降低長文脈 LLM 推論的記憶體瓶頸，進一步壓低大規模部署成本。
