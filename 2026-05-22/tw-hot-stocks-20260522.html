Two catalysts collided: NVIDIA's blowout Q1 FY27 earnings ($81.6B revenue, +85% YoY; Q2 guidance $91B) renewed AI supply-chain confidence, while massive pent-up buying pressure from the suspension period — over 290,000 pending buy orders — drove an instant limit-up opening.
